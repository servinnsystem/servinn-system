SERVINN User Agreement

Version: November 14, 2026 (revision 2)

Welcome to SERVINN — A Secured and Reliable Cross-Matching Employment System.

By creating an account, accessing, or using SERVINN, you agree to follow the terms and conditions stated in this User Agreement. Please read these provisions carefully before using our platform.

1. PURPOSE OF SERVINN

SERVINN is an online employment-matching platform designed to connect employers with individuals seeking employment opportunities, particularly short-term, part-time, and service-based jobs.

Our platform aims to:

  • Connect qualified workers with suitable job opportunities.
  • Help employers find individuals with relevant skills.
  • Provide a secure and convenient employment-matching environment.
  • Support communication between employers and job seekers.
  • Promote transparency, trust, and fair employment opportunities.

2. USER ELIGIBILITY AND ACCOUNT REGISTRATION

To use SERVINN, users must:

  • Provide accurate, complete, and truthful registration information.
  • Use a valid email address.
  • Create and maintain a secure password.
  • Submit the required information for identity verification when requested.
  • Meet applicable legal age and eligibility requirements.

Users must not create accounts using another person's identity or provide misleading information.

SERVINN reserves the right to restrict, suspend, or reject accounts containing false or suspicious information.

3. USER ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

A. Employers

Employers agree to:

  • Provide accurate job descriptions and employment details.
  • Clearly state job responsibilities, qualifications, location, schedule, and compensation.
  • Treat applicants fairly and professionally.
  • Communicate honestly with job seekers.
  • Honor the employment terms mutually agreed upon.
  • Avoid fraudulent, discriminatory, misleading, or unlawful job postings.

B. Employees / Job Seekers

Job seekers agree to:

  • Provide accurate personal and professional information.
  • Present genuine skills, qualifications, and experience.
  • Apply only to jobs they are genuinely interested in.
  • Communicate respectfully with employers.
  • Fulfill the responsibilities they have agreed to undertake.
  • Avoid fraudulent applications and misrepresentation.

C. Administrators

Administrators are responsible for:

  • Reviewing account registrations and verification submissions.
  • Approving or rejecting accounts based on platform requirements.
  • Monitoring job postings and reported activities.
  • Managing inappropriate or fraudulent content.
  • Maintaining the proper operation and security of the platform.

4. IDENTITY VERIFICATION AND ACCOUNT SECURITY

SERVINN may require users to submit valid identification documents to verify their identities.

Users acknowledge that:

  • Submitted identification information must be authentic.
  • Verification does not guarantee that a user will be trustworthy or qualified for every job.
  • Identity documents will be handled according to the platform's Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws.
  • Users must protect their login credentials and immediately report suspected unauthorized account access.

SERVINN may suspend accounts when identity information is inconsistent, fraudulent, or suspicious.

5. JOB POSTINGS AND APPLICATIONS

Employers must ensure that their job postings contain accurate and relevant information.

Job seekers understand that:

  • Job recommendations are generated based on available profile information and matching criteria.
  • A matching score is an indication of compatibility, not a guarantee of employment.
  • Applying for a job does not guarantee acceptance.
  • Employers retain responsibility for their hiring decisions.

SERVINN may remove expired, misleading, inappropriate, or fraudulent job listings.

6. INTELLIGENT JOB-MATCHING SYSTEM

SERVINN uses an intelligent cross-matching mechanism to recommend job opportunities based on information such as:

  • Skills and qualifications.
  • Preferred location.
  • Employment type.
  • Relevant experience.
  • Job requirements and availability.

Users understand that matching results are recommendations and should not be treated as guarantees of employment or qualification.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their profile information is updated and accurate.

7. COMMUNICATION AND EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENTS

SERVINN provides communication features that allow employers and job seekers to discuss employment opportunities.

Both parties are responsible for:

  • Communicating honestly and respectfully.
  • Confirming job responsibilities before starting work.
  • Discussing compensation, working hours, schedules, and other relevant conditions.
  • Reviewing and mutually agreeing upon employment terms.
  • Keeping appropriate records of their agreed arrangements.

Neither party should assume that an employment agreement exists until the relevant terms have been mutually accepted.

8. PAYMENT AND COMPENSATION

Employers and job seekers must clearly discuss and agree on compensation before work begins.

The agreed payment should specify:

  • Total amount or rate of compensation.
  • Payment schedule or frequency.
  • Method of payment.
  • Any additional conditions mutually accepted by both parties.

Unless SERVINN explicitly provides an authorized payment-processing service, compensation is arranged directly between the employer and worker.

SERVINN does not guarantee payment or independently resolve every financial disagreement between users.

9. RATINGS AND REVIEWS

SERVINN may provide a rating and review system to encourage accountability and transparency.

Users agree to:

  • Submit honest and respectful feedback.
  • Avoid fake, misleading, abusive, or retaliatory reviews.
  • Rate other users based on genuine employment experiences.

Ratings should only be submitted for genuine interactions or completed work, according to platform requirements.

10. PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES

Users are prohibited from:

  • Creating fake accounts or impersonating others.
  • Publishing fraudulent or misleading job opportunities.
  • Requesting unauthorized payments or suspicious fees.
  • Harassing, threatening, or discriminating against other users.
  • Sharing another person's private information without authorization.
  • Attempting to access restricted accounts or system resources.
  • Uploading malicious files or attempting to compromise platform security.
  • Using SERVINN for unlawful activities.

Violations may result in warnings, account suspension, or permanent termination.

11. PRIVACY AND DATA PROTECTION

SERVINN respects the privacy of its users.

Personal information, identification documents, contact details, and account information will be collected, processed, stored, and protected in accordance with the SERVINN Privacy Policy and applicable Philippine data protection laws, including the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Users are encouraged to review the Privacy Policy before submitting personal information.

12. ACCOUNT SUSPENSION AND TERMINATION

SERVINN reserves the right to restrict, suspend, or terminate accounts that violate this agreement or compromise platform security.

Users may request account closure through the platform's designated account-management process.

Account closure does not automatically remove information that must be retained under applicable legal obligations or for legitimate documented purposes.

13. LIMITATION OF RESPONSIBILITY

SERVINN provides a platform for connecting employers and job seekers.

Although the platform implements identity verification and job-matching features, SERVINN does not guarantee:

  • That every employer or job seeker will act honestly.
  • That every job posting will result in employment.
  • That users will successfully complete their employment arrangements.
  • That all disputes between employers and workers will be resolved through the platform.

Nothing in this agreement removes rights or obligations that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

14. CHANGES TO THIS AGREEMENT

SERVINN may update this User Agreement when necessary to reflect changes in platform functionality, policies, or applicable legal requirements.

Users will be notified of material changes through appropriate platform communication.

Continued use after the effective date of an updated agreement will be subject to applicable notice and acceptance requirements.

15. CONTACT AND SUPPORT

For questions, concerns, complaints, or reports regarding this agreement, users may contact the SERVINN administration team through the official platform communication channels.

16. USER ACKNOWLEDGMENT AND CONSENT

By selecting the acceptance checkbox and creating an account, I acknowledge that:

  • I have read and understood the SERVINN User Agreement.
  • I agree to follow the platform's rules, policies, and requirements.
  • I confirm that the information I provide is accurate and truthful.
  • I understand my responsibilities as an employer or job seeker.
  • I acknowledge that my personal information will be processed according to the Privacy Policy.
  • I understand that violations of this agreement may result in account restrictions or termination.
