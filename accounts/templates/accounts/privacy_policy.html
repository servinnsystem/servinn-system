Your information, explained

Applicant Data Privacy Policy

Before you create a profile, upload documents or apply for a job, read how SERVINN collects, uses and shares your information.

Updated November 20, 2026 · Version {{ policy_version }}

Your information supports account verification, job matching and the applications you choose to submit. Administrators review identity documents. Employers receive relevant applicant details for their own job postings. You can review your profile and ask about access, correction or deletion.

2. How your information is used

  • Create and protect your account: sign you in, verify an email address and help prevent unauthorized access.
  • Verify your identity: authorized administrators review your submitted details, identity documents and clearances, then approve your profile or ask for corrections.
  • Find relevant jobs: compare your skills, location and work preferences with available job postings.
  • Process your applications: show relevant application and profile information to the employer whose job you applied for, and display their decisions in your application tracker.
  • Send service emails: deliver verification codes and notify you when an employer accepts your application, using your registered email address.
  • Operate and protect SERVINN: manage saved jobs and ratings, moderate abusive text, review reports and produce administrative statistics.

Your ID number, ID images and selfie are collected for verification, not to calculate your profile compatibility score. Avoid including unrelated sensitive information in your introduction or job-application communications.

3. Who can see your information

  • You: you can view and update the profile information available in Profile Settings and manage your documents and applications.
  • Authorized SERVINN administrators: administrators can review account details, uploaded verification documents, job postings and application activity for verification, support, moderation and service administration.
  • Employers you apply to: their applicant-review page shows your name, email, contact number, location, relevant qualifications, application details and profile verification status. Employers can use these details to assess your application and contact you about the job.
  • Other users: job postings can show aggregate job ratings. SERVINN does not publish an applicant directory that exposes every applicant's identity documents.

Government ID images, selfies and clearance files are kept in private document storage. The current document-download controls allow the account owner and authorized administrators; employers are not granted direct access to these files through the applicant-review page.

When you apply, the application keeps a copy of your name and the job/employer details at that time. The employer may also see your current profile qualifications when reviewing the application. An employer may keep information or correspondence outside SERVINN; changes or deletion within SERVINN do not automatically remove those separate records or emails.

4. Profile compatibility and employer decisions

SERVINN calculates a suggested compatibility score using profile and job information such as skills, location and employment preferences. The score helps order or highlight opportunities. It does not automatically hire or reject you, verify every qualification, or guarantee an interview or job. Employers review applications and make their hiring decisions.

5. Services, storage and safeguards

SERVINN uses Cloud Firestore for account and employment records, private server storage for uploaded files, Google OAuth for Google sign-in, and a configured email provider for verification and acceptance messages. Google receives the sign-in request and information needed for authentication; the email provider processes the email address and message being delivered. Some pages also load fonts from Google.

Providers may process information outside the Philippines depending on the hosting region and service used. Their own privacy terms also apply to their services.

Access checks limit account and document access. Passwords are stored as hashes, and saved service credentials are encrypted. These measures reduce risk but do not make any online service risk-free. Keep your account password and email codes private, and sign out on shared devices.

6. How long information is kept

Account profiles, verification records, applications and related activity remain stored unless they are removed through available controls or an administrator handles a removal request. SERVINN does not currently apply a fixed automatic deletion period to all records. Closing a job, signing out or stopping use of the service does not automatically delete your profile, uploaded documents or past applications.

Private recovery backups may contain earlier copies. A removal request should cover the relevant account records, files and backups; the administrator should explain any information that must be retained and the reason. Emails already delivered and records kept separately by an employer cannot be recalled automatically.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

Read the form labels before submitting. Fields marked as required are needed for the associated account, verification or application step; optional fields can be left blank. You can choose email/password sign-in instead of Google and decide which jobs to apply for.

Review and correct editable information through Profile Settings, and manage replacements through Documents & corrections. These account pages require sign-in.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to or a copy of your personal data, correction, restriction or deletion, or object to processing and withdraw consent where processing depends on consent. Contact the administrator to make a request. Identity verification may be needed before information is disclosed or changed. Some requests may affect the account or employment services available to you.

You may also raise a concern with the Philippine National Privacy Commission.
